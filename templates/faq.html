Getting Started

Reading & Library

Our manga reader supports vertical scroll (webtoon style) and single/double page layouts. It automatically remembers your progress. You can switch reading modes in the settings menu while reading.

The Watchlist tracks what you're reading. Statuses include: Planned, Reading, Completed, On Hold, and Dropped. We automatically update a book to "Reading" when you start it.
Publishing Content

You can select the "Publisher" role during registration. If you already have a Reader account, you can request a role upgrade via the "Help & Info" menu or by contacting support.

  • Books: PDF files (Standard text/images)
  • Manga: PDF per chapter (Images embedded in PDF)
  • Audio: MP3 files for audiobooks
  • Covers: JPG, PNG, WEBP (Max 5MB)
Monetization & Rewards

Publishers earn money by fulfilling content requests.
  • Base Reward: $0.50 per fulfilled book/manga request.
  • Chapter Reward: $0.15 per specific chapter request.
  • Bonus: +$0.20 for every 2 upvotes the request had!

Earnings are tracked in your dashboard. Payouts are processed monthly for balances over $20. Ensure your payout details are updated in your settings.
